# Glossary

Every Storyliner word this guide uses, in a sentence each, with the page that explains it. The other pages link here when they use a word they don't stop to explain.

## Find a word

1. Look under the word's first letter below.
2. Read its sentence.
3. Follow the arrow (→) to the page that explains it, with pictures.

## A

**Add dialogue to** — a node that puts an option on a character's wheel when the flow reaches it, and waits there until the player picks it; then the conversation it leads to plays. It's how a quest giver offers a quest. → [Quests, events and triggers](quests-and-events.md)

**Add scene to** — a node that puts a scene on a place's scene list from a quest, and waits there until the scene starts. → [Locations and scene lists](locations.md)

**Already there** — a scene that starts quietly once it's on a place's list and its condition holds, and goes on until it ends, putting its options on people's wheels. → [Locations and scene lists](locations.md)

**Arrow** — the line that joins two nodes on a canvas, in the order they play; also called a wire. → [Canvases, nodes and wires](canvases.md)

**Assignment** — what an Execution node does: a fact set to a value, such as *winkel_invited_you = true*. → [Facts, conditions and world states](facts-and-conditions.md)

**Auto-arrange** — lays out the selected nodes, or the whole canvas, left to right in the order the arrows run (downwards, with *Top to bottom* on). → [Canvases, nodes and wires](canvases.md)

**Autosave** — a copy of your unsaved work that Storyliner keeps every few minutes beside the project, so nothing is lost if the computer stops. → [Getting started](getting-started.md)

**Available** — a quest's state when the player can start it: its giver's options are on offer. → [Quests, events and triggers](quests-and-events.md)

## B

**Badge** — the **!** (a warning) or **×** (an error) at a node's top right when the validator found something there. → [The validator](validator.md)

**Band** — a game phase drawn across the storylines on the project map, or the stripe of a phase's colour across the top of a node inside its box. → [Phases](phases.md)

**Bottom panel** — the panel under the canvas, with the Search, Problems and Used by tabs. → [Getting started](getting-started.md)

**Box** — a phase drawn on the canvas it belongs to, around the nodes the flow reaches while it's on. → [Phases](phases.md)

**Breadcrumb** — the path above a canvas — storyline › situation › dialogue — that says where you are and how you got there. → [Canvases, nodes and wires](canvases.md)

**Break Hub** — a node that leaves the hub around it, wired from an option such as *Goodbye*. → [Writing dialogue](dialogue.md)

**Break open** — brings a placed situation's contents out onto the canvas it's on, wired where its pins were. → [Canvases, nodes and wires](canvases.md)

## C

**Canvas** — where you build the story, inside a storyline, a situation or a dialogue: nodes joined by arrows. → [Canvases, nodes and wires](canvases.md)

**Character** — someone in the story, with a sheet: his lines, his wheel, his entry lines and one-liners. → [Characters](characters.md)

**Character view** — a character's sheet opened at *Everything he says*. → [Reading tools](reading-tools.md)

**Choice** — a node that offers the player options, each leading its own way. → [Writing dialogue](dialogue.md)

**Code name** — the name conditions, assignments and the game use for a fact, a quest or anything else: no spaces, like *escaped_from_jail*. → [Facts, conditions and world states](facts-and-conditions.md)

**Codex** — the game's encyclopedia of people, places and things: pages that unlock as the story goes. → [The journal, the codex and interface text](journal.md)

**Comment** — a note on a canvas or an element: a design note, a TODO, a direction for the voice actor — each kind a comment category. → [Canvases, nodes and wires](canvases.md)

**Condition** — a check on facts, such as *player_is_wanted and not escaped_from_jail*; a Condition node sends the flow one way or another by it. → [Facts, conditions and world states](facts-and-conditions.md)

**Coverage grid** — characters and locations, or storylines, down the side, phases across the top: how much of each reacts in each phase. → [Phases](phases.md)

## D

**Debug panel** — the part of the play window that changes any fact, phase or quest state, and steps back and forward. → [Play mode and the playable HTML file](play-mode.md)

**Details panel** — the panel on the right that shows and edits whatever is selected. → [Getting started](getting-started.md)

**Dialogue** — a conversation: lines, choices and hubs on a canvas of its own, inside a situation or on a character. → [Writing dialogue](dialogue.md)

## E

**Editor settings** — your own settings, for you on this computer: autosaves, undo, the panels, touchpad or mouse, where the play window was. → [Files, settings and keyboard shortcuts](files-and-settings.md)

**Engine event** — something the story tells the game to do — play a sound, open a shop — logged by play mode, not run. → [Quests, events and triggers](quests-and-events.md)

**Entry line** — what a character says when the player starts talking to him, before his wheel; picked from his pools. → [Characters](characters.md)

**Entry pin, exit pin** — the ways into and out of a canvas: a situation is entered through an entry pin and left through an exit pin. → [Canvases, nodes and wires](canvases.md)

**Error** — a validator finding that means the story can't run as written; it can't be suppressed. → [The validator](validator.md)

**Execution** — a node that sets facts as the flow passes. → [Facts, conditions and world states](facts-and-conditions.md)

## F

**Fact** — something the story remembers: yes or no, or a number, such as *knows_the_password* or *gold*. → [Facts, conditions and world states](facts-and-conditions.md)

**Fact list** — a named list that keeps related facts together in the Facts tab. → [Facts, conditions and world states](facts-and-conditions.md)

**Fact view** — one fact in a tab: what it starts as, everywhere it's set, everywhere it's read. → [Reading tools](reading-tools.md)

**Faction** — a group people belong to, with a reputation the story can raise or lower. → [Facts, conditions and world states](facts-and-conditions.md)

**Field** — one piece of information a template adds to things — a price, a mood, a portrait. → [Templates and fields](templates.md)

**Field group** — fields that belong together, such as *Shop*, added to templates as one. → [Templates and fields](templates.md)

**Finding** — one thing the validator found: an error or a warning, with where it is and how to fix it. → [The validator](validator.md)

**Frame** — the dashed box around everything on a canvas: the storyline, situation or dialogue you're in, with its entry and exit pins on it. → [Canvases, nodes and wires](canvases.md)

## G

**Game panel** — the left of the play window: what the game would do — where the player is, who's here, what's waiting. → [Play mode and the playable HTML file](play-mode.md)

**Game value** — a value the game fills in when a line plays, such as *{player_name}*; play mode shows its test value. → [Writing dialogue](dialogue.md)

**Generic character** — one sheet for many NPCs, such as every guard; each NPC remembers his own conversations. → [Characters](characters.md)

**Ghost** — a faded box beside a frame's pin that shows what's on the other side of that door, on the canvas around: what leads in, or where the way out goes. Double-click it to go there. → [Canvases, nodes and wires](canvases.md)

## H

**Hub** — a menu the player comes back to after each option, until he leaves: *ask about anything*. → [Writing dialogue](dialogue.md)

## I

**ID** — the hidden, permanent name Storyliner gives everything it makes, so renaming a thing changes nothing that points at it. → [Facts, conditions and world states](facts-and-conditions.md)

**Import kit** — the guides that let an AI agent turn another tool's quest into an outline, and the tool it runs to make a project of it. → [Importing](importing.md)

**Interaction point** — the code name of a thing in the level — a notice board, a door — that the player uses to start a *when used* scene. → [Locations and scene lists](locations.md)

**Interface text** — the game's own words — menus, the HUD, tutorials — in text tables, ready to be translated with the rest when translations come. → [The journal, the codex and interface text](journal.md)

## J

**Journal** — the player's quest log: each quest with its objectives and entries, as the story writes them. → [The journal, the codex and interface text](journal.md)

**Journal node** — a node that writes to the journal as the flow passes: a quest shown, an entry added, an objective shown, done or failed, a quest finished or failed, a codex page unlocked. → [The journal, the codex and interface text](journal.md)

## K

**Kind** — what a thing in the Library is: a character, a location, a faction, or a kind you add, such as *Item*. → [Templates and fields](templates.md)

## L

**Level** — what a phase belongs to: the game, a storyline, a situation or a dialogue. → [Phases](phases.md)

**Library** — the tab of the left panel that lists characters, locations, factions and your own kinds of thing. → [The project and the project map](project-and-map.md)

**Line** — one thing someone says, with its speaker, its words, and any variants. → [Writing dialogue](dialogue.md)

**Line ID** — the permanent code each piece of player-facing text gets, so its translations and recordings can follow it once those come. → [Templates and fields](templates.md)

**Location** — a place in the game world, one inside another: a city, a tavern, a back room. → [Locations and scene lists](locations.md)

**Lock** — what stops the same project being edited in two windows at once: the second opens read-only. → [Getting started](getting-started.md)

## M

**Mapping file** — the file an import writes beside the project, listing each of the other tool's IDs beside the new one. → [Importing](importing.md)

**Markup** — the marks inside a line's words: emphasis, a pause, a game value. → [Writing dialogue](dialogue.md)

**Menu text** — the words a menu shows for an option: a wheel's options always have one, and a Choice's or a Hub's do when *Menu text on options* is on in the project settings — written then on the line the option leads to. → [Writing dialogue](dialogue.md)

**Minimap** — the small picture of the whole canvas at its bottom right. → [Canvases, nodes and wires](canvases.md)

## N

**Narrator** — the speaker of lines nobody in the story says: descriptions, cutscene text. → [Writing dialogue](dialogue.md)

**Node** — one step on a canvas: a line, a choice, a condition, a placed situation and the rest. → [Canvases, nodes and wires](canvases.md)

**NPC** — a non-player character: anyone in the game the player doesn't control. → [Characters](characters.md)

## O

**Objective** — a task a journal quest lists for the player, done or failed as the story goes. → [The journal, the codex and interface text](journal.md)

**Occasion** — a moment the game asks a character for a one-liner, besides passing by and on interaction — *spotted*, *combat_start*. → [Characters](characters.md)

**One-liner** — a single line a character says without a conversation: passing by, or when he won't talk. → [Characters](characters.md)

**Only during, not during** — the chips on a sheet that say in which phases something is offered: a character's options, entry lines, one-liners and pools, and a place's scenes. → [Phases](phases.md)

**Outline** — a plain text file, *Name.outline.md*, that describes a quest by names; Storyliner makes a project of it. → [Importing](importing.md)

## P

**Phase** — a period of the game — an act, a season, a war — that's on or off. → [Phases](phases.md)

**Phase diff** — two phases side by side, showing only what differs between them. → [Phases](phases.md)

**Phase switch** — a node that sends the flow one way if a phase is on, another if it isn't; with several phases, out by the first one that's on, else by *Other*. → [Phases](phases.md)

**Phase view** — the canvas shown as it would read in one world state, what that state rules out faded. → [Phases](phases.md)

**Pin** — a point on a node or a frame where an arrow starts or ends; see entry pin. → [Canvases, nodes and wires](canvases.md)

**Placement** — a situation put on a canvas; the same situation can be placed in several places. → [The project and the project map](project-and-map.md)

**Play mode** — playing your own quest, with the real logic running, in a window of its own. → [Play mode and the playable HTML file](play-mode.md)

**Play window** — play mode's own window, beside the editor's. → [Play mode and the playable HTML file](play-mode.md)

**Playable HTML file** — one file holding the project and the play screen, that opens in any browser with nothing installed. → [Play mode and the playable HTML file](play-mode.md)

**Player-facing text** — words the player reads or hears: lines, options, journal and codex text, interface text. Each piece has a line ID. → [Templates and fields](templates.md)

**Pool** — a group of a character's lines, with a condition, from which one is picked: random or in order. → [Characters](characters.md)

**Problems** — the bottom panel's tab that lists the validator's findings. → [The validator](validator.md)

**Project** — everything about your game's story, kept in one *.quest* file in its own folder. → [The project and the project map](project-and-map.md)

**Project map** — every storyline at once, with the arrows between them: what unlocks what. → [The project and the project map](project-and-map.md)

**Project settings** — settings kept in the project, for everyone: its name, default timing, menu text on options, how much of a node must be inside a box, where the player starts, the checks switched off. → [Files, settings and keyboard shortcuts](files-and-settings.md)

## Q

**Quest** — a storyline the player takes on, with a state: unavailable, available, taken, finished or failed. → [Quests, events and triggers](quests-and-events.md)

**Quest fact** — a fact that belongs to one quest, written *quest.QuestName.fact_name*. → [Facts, conditions and world states](facts-and-conditions.md)

**Quest node** — a node that changes a quest's state: takes it, ends it, makes it available or not. → [Quests, events and triggers](quests-and-events.md)

## R

**Remove dialogue from** — a node that takes things off a character's sheet for the rest of the playthrough: options on his wheel, entry lines, one-liners, whole pools. → [Quests, events and triggers](quests-and-events.md)

**Report** — what an import did: what came in and what came out, what needs your hand, what the checks found. → [Importing](importing.md)

**Reputation** — how a faction feels about the player: a number the story raises and lowers. → [Facts, conditions and world states](facts-and-conditions.md)

**Retire** — what deleting a fact does: it moves to its list's *Retired* section, where nothing new can use it and old game saves still recognise it. → [Facts, conditions and world states](facts-and-conditions.md)

**Run** — one playthrough in play mode, kept as it goes, so it's picked up where you left it. → [Play mode and the playable HTML file](play-mode.md)

## S

**Scene** — something on a place's scene list — a small flow of the place's own, or one a quest adds: it plays on arrival, when used, or is already there. → [Locations and scene lists](locations.md)

**Scene list** — what can happen at a place, in the order the game reads it. → [Locations and scene lists](locations.md)

**Script view** — a dialogue read top to bottom, like a screenplay. → [Reading tools](reading-tools.md)

**Search** — the bottom panel's tab that finds any word or name, anywhere in the project (Ctrl+F). → [Reading tools](reading-tools.md)

**Section** — a labelled frame around nodes on a canvas, to group them; it changes nothing in the flow. → [Canvases, nodes and wires](canvases.md)

**Sheet** — a character's page: his details, his wheel, his entry lines and one-liners, everything he says. → [Characters](characters.md)

**Situation** — a scene of the story — at the customs, in the tavern — that a storyline places; it holds dialogues and other nodes. → [The project and the project map](project-and-map.md)

**Speaker** — who says a line: a character, *Player*, *Narrator*, or *UI* for words shown on screen; a place's lines can also be said by *Anyone*. → [Writing dialogue](dialogue.md)

**Starter** — a ready-made piece of story — nodes and arrows, such as a merchant's conversation — that you insert as a fresh copy, on the same kind of canvas it was made on. → [Templates and fields](templates.md)

**Storyline** — a whole thread of the story, often a quest, with its situations in order on its canvas. → [The project and the project map](project-and-map.md)

**Storyline view** — a whole storyline read straight through, like a document. → [Reading tools](reading-tools.md)

**Strip** — a phase from above — the game's, or an outer storyline's or situation's — drawn along the top of a canvas's frame. → [Phases](phases.md)

**Suppress** — hide one warning from your own list, with a note saying why it's fine; a teammate still sees it. → [The validator](validator.md)

## T

**Taken** — a quest's state once the player has started it. → [Quests, events and triggers](quests-and-events.md)

**Template** — what a kind of thing is made of: its field groups and fields, such as *Merchant* for characters who trade. → [Templates and fields](templates.md)

**Text table** — a table of interface text — *Main menu*, *HUD* — each row a key and its words. → [The journal, the codex and interface text](journal.md)

**Timing** — how long a line stays before the next one, in seconds, and any pause after it. → [Writing dialogue](dialogue.md)

**Top to bottom** — a way of showing canvases and the project map with the flow running downwards instead of to the right. → [Canvases, nodes and wires](canvases.md)

**Track** — a set of phases never on together: starting one ends whichever of its track was on. → [Phases](phases.md)

**Trigger** — something the game tells the story has happened — the player picked the lock — that a *Wait for trigger* node waits for. → [Quests, events and triggers](quests-and-events.md)

## U

**Undo** — takes back the last change (Ctrl+Z); **redo** puts it back (Ctrl+Y). → [Getting started](getting-started.md)

**Used by** — the bottom panel's tab that lists everywhere the selected thing is used. → [Reading tools](reading-tools.md)

## V

**Validator** — the checks Storyliner runs on your project as you edit, finding what can't run and what looks like a hole. → [The validator](validator.md)

**Variant** — another version of a line that plays instead when its label — a condition or a phase — is true. → [Writing dialogue](dialogue.md)

**Version control** — the tool a team shares files through and keeps their history in, such as Diversion. → [Files, settings and keyboard shortcuts](files-and-settings.md)

## W

**Wait for trigger** — a node where the flow waits until the game fires a trigger. → [Quests, events and triggers](quests-and-events.md)

**Warning** — a validator finding that looks like a hole but doesn't stop the story; it can be suppressed with a note. → [The validator](validator.md)

**Wheel** — the menu of things to talk about with a character, offered after his entry line. → [Characters](characters.md)

**When used** — a scene that waits until the player uses its interaction point. → [Locations and scene lists](locations.md)

**Wire** — see Arrow. → [Canvases, nodes and wires](canvases.md)

**World state** — a moment of the game named and kept — which phases are on, which facts are true — to look at, check and play in. → [Facts, conditions and world states](facts-and-conditions.md)

## Details and tips

- Words for what's still coming — translations, voice-over, documents — aren't here yet: they come with the versions that build them. The [Guide home](README.md) lists what's coming.
- Each sentence is short on purpose: the page its arrow points to has the steps and the pictures.
